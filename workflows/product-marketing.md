# Product Marketing Lifecycle
1. Ask the Market Research Agent for current competitor positioning, customer pain points, and evidence-backed product gaps.
2. Confirm product facts, target market, audience, value proposition, and claims substantiation.
3. Marketing agent prepares positioning, competitor review, messaging, launch plan, content calendar, and channel strategy.
4. Presentation agent prepares customer deck, demo script, one-pager, and proposal.
5. Humanization agent edits prose for naturalness and localization without changing facts.
6. CTO/Product Manager verify product accuracy and brand consistency.
7. Present campaign and publishing plan for user approval.
8. Publish or spend only after explicit approval and through authorized integrations.
9. Measure results and recommend ongoing improvements.
