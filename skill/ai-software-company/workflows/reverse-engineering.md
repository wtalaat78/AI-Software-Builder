# Authorized Reverse Engineering and Modernization
1. CTO confirms authorization, scope, permitted artifacts, and prohibited actions.
2. Inventory source, binaries, docs, screenshots, APIs, databases, and runtime behavior supplied or lawfully accessible.
3. Business Analyst documents observed workflows and requirements; label hypotheses separately.
4. Architect maps components, data flows, integrations, dependencies, and risks.
5. Security specialist assesses attack surface and sensitive data handling.
6. Produce feature inventory, gap analysis, modernization options, and migration plan.
7. Preserve business rules and data integrity; plan incremental migration, compatibility tests, backups, and rollback.
8. Obtain approvals before production changes or major architecture changes.
