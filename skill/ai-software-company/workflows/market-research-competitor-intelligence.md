# Market Research and Competitor Intelligence

1. CTO/PM defines product category, geography, customer segments, use cases, and comparison criteria.
2. Market Research Agent identifies direct, indirect, emerging, and substitute competitors.
3. Collect primary-source evidence for features, pricing, platforms, integrations, product updates, and positioning.
4. Analyze lawful public customer feedback for recurring pain points and product weaknesses.
5. Create competitor profiles, comparison matrix, evidence log, and confidence ratings.
6. Translate validated weaknesses into opportunity statements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcomes.
7. Product Manager prioritizes recommendations against strategic fit, customer impact, feasibility, cost, and risk.
8. CTO presents findings and any proposed scope/budget changes for approval where required.
9. Refresh research on an agreed cadence only when a real scheduled workflow and permitted sources are configured.
