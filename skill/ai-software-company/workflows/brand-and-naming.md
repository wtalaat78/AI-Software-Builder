# Brand Naming and Visual Identity Lifecycle

1. CTO and Product Manager define product category, core value proposition, target customer persona, and geographic markets (e.g. MENA, US, Global).
2. Market Research Agent supplies competitor naming trends, brand archetypes, and market whitespace opportunities.
3. Brand Identity Designer generates 3-5 distinct product naming candidates:
   - Root word analysis, phonetic harmony, memorability, transliteration, and Arabic RTL / English LTR compatibility.
   - Initial screening for domain, handle, and basic trademark clearance risk flags.
4. CTO and Product Manager review and select the primary name candidate.
5. Brand Identity Designer formulates the Visual Brand System:
   - Logo concept specifications (symbol, wordmark, combination mark).
   - Clear generation briefs for Antigravity's `image-generator` subagent to produce visual logo prototypes and icons.
   - Comprehensive color palette with light and dark mode tokens and WCAG 2.1 AA contrast compliance.
   - Typography pairing (Arabic calligraphy/typography + English body and display fonts).
   - App icon matrix (Web Favicon 16x16/32x32/48x48, iOS 1024x1024, Android 512x512 + adaptive vector).
6. Humanization Specialist reviews brand narrative and tagline for clarity, naturalness, and cultural authenticity.
7. Brand Identity Designer packages the Brand Identity Brief and design tokens for the UI/UX Designer, Frontend Developer, Mobile Developer, and Product Marketing Manager.
